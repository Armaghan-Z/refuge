Career Development

Build your skills.

Free workshops, coaching, and programs designed to help you present your best self to employers.

Resume Writing Workshop

Learn how to translate international experience into a U.S.-style resume. Offered by IRC San Diego — free for refugees.

Interview Preparation

Mock interviews, coaching on common questions, and guidance on professional etiquette for U.S. workplace culture.

Skills Training Programs

Free vocational training in healthcare, IT, construction trades, and culinary arts through Workforce Partnership San Diego.

Refugee Employer Network

Companies in San Diego that actively participate in refugee hiring programs, with dedicated HR contacts for newcomers.

Mentorship Program

Be matched with a professional mentor in your field who can provide career guidance, networking, and support.

Credential Recognition Help

Guidance on getting international degrees and certifications recognized in the U.S. — including evaluation services and supplemental courses.

The Refuge.

Empowering Lives, Bridging Communities. A prototype resource hub for refugees in San Diego.
