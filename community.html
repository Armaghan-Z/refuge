Local Organizations

Organizations serving San Diego.

These are real, active organizations with verified addresses and websites that serve refugees in the San Diego area.

IRC
International Rescue Committee
5348 University Ave, San Diego 92105
Housing Employment Education Legal Aid Health

The IRC is one of the largest refugee resettlement agencies in San Diego, providing direct services from housing placement to job training and long-term integration support.

Visit Website (619) 234-3040
JFS
Jewish Family Service
8788 Balboa Ave, San Diego 92123
Legal Aid Food Assistance Mental Health Employment

JFS provides comprehensive services including free immigration legal aid, emergency food assistance, mental health counseling, and employment support — open to people of all backgrounds.

Visit Website (858) 872-0101
AAA
Alliance for African Assistance
5952 El Cajon Blvd, San Diego 92115
Cultural Programs Translation Job Training Education

Specializing in serving African and other underserved refugee communities, AAA provides culturally responsive case management, translation services, job training, and educational support.

Visit Website (619) 265-7683
