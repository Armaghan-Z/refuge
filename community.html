Community

You are not alone in San Diego

San Diego is home to one of the most welcoming and diverse refugee communities in the United States. These organizations are here to help you find your people, your culture, and your place.

🏠 Resettlement Agencies 🌍 Cultural Orgs 🕌 Faith Communities 👶 Children & Youth 👩 Women 📚 Libraries

Refugee Resettlement Agencies

The three main resettlement agencies in San Diego. If you are newly arrived, one of these is likely already your case manager. If you are not connected to any of them, call IRC first.

International Rescue Committee (IRC)

Resettlement Agency

The IRC has operated in San Diego since the 1970s and serves more refugees than any other organization in the county. Services include: housing, employment, legal aid, ESL, mental health, medical case management, financial coaching, and community programs. The IRC is often the first contact for newly arrived refugees.

(619) 641-7510
5348 University Ave, San Diego, CA 92105
Free · Full-service resettlement

Catholic Charities of San Diego

Resettlement Agency

Provides comprehensive refugee resettlement services, immigration legal services, housing assistance, employment services, and social support programs. Serves people of all backgrounds and faiths. BIA-accredited immigration legal services available at low cost.

(619) 287-9454 ext. 2100
4575-A Mission Gorge Place, San Diego
Free · Full-service resettlement

JFS San Diego / HIAS

Resettlement Agency

Jewish Family Service in partnership with HIAS provides resettlement services for refugees and asylum seekers of all backgrounds. Services include case management, employment, ESL, mental health counseling, and community integration.

(858) 872-0101
8788 Balboa Ave, San Diego, CA 92123
Free · Full-service resettlement

Cultural & Community Organizations

Organizations serving specific communities and regions of origin within San Diego's diverse refugee population.

Alliance for African Assistance

African Refugee Community

Dedicated to helping African refugees and immigrants in San Diego build self-sufficiency. Services include refugee resettlement, employment services, ESL, immigration legal help, and culturally specific programs. Strong focus on East African communities including Somali, Sudanese, Ethiopian, and Eritrean communities.

(619) 265-7683
5952 El Cajon Blvd, San Diego
Free · African communities

World Relief San Diego

Multi-Service Community Organization

Faith-based organization providing resettlement, employment, immigration legal services, and English classes for refugees and immigrants from all backgrounds. Known for strong community integration programs and micro-enterprise support for refugee entrepreneurs.

socalsandiego@wr.org
4858 Mercury St Suite 202, San Diego
Free · All backgrounds

Survivors of Torture International

Trauma & Healing Community

Provides specialized rehabilitation for torture survivors including group therapy, community programs, and advocacy. Their community programs bring together survivors from many backgrounds to share experiences and support each other's healing.

(619) 278-2400
Free · Trauma survivors

ACOF — Afghan Community of San Diego

Afghan Refugee Community

Serves San Diego's large Afghan refugee and immigrant community with cultural programs, language support (Dari and Pashto), community events, and connections to Afghan-specific services. San Diego's Afghan community is one of the largest in the U.S.

Contact via IRC: (619) 641-7510
Free · Afghan community

Faith Communities & Places of Worship

San Diego has vibrant faith communities serving diverse refugee populations. Many offer practical services alongside spiritual community.

ℹ
Finding your community

San Diego has mosques, churches, and other places of worship serving Arabic-speaking, Somali, Afghan, Sudanese, Karen, Burmese, and many other refugee communities. IRC and Alliance for African Assistance can connect you with specific faith communities based on your background and language.

Islamic Center of San Diego

Mosque & Community Center

One of San Diego's largest mosques with active programs for refugees and immigrants. Offers emergency assistance, zakat distribution, food pantry, and community events. Serves Arabic, Somali, Afghan, and other Muslim communities.

(619) 583-8555
7050 Eckstrom Ave, San Diego

Episcopal Community Services

Multi-Faith Community Services

Provides emergency shelter, food pantry, and social services for refugees and low-income families regardless of faith background. Known for welcoming services to people of all backgrounds.

(619) 236-9091

Children & Youth Programs

Free programs for refugee children and teens to learn, play, and build friendships.

IRC Youth Program

After-School & Youth Programs

IRC provides after-school tutoring, sports, arts, and social programs for refugee children and teens. Connects youth with mentors and college preparation resources. Available for children ages 5–18.

(619) 641-7510
Free

San Diego Unified — Newcomer Program

School Integration Support

San Diego Unified School District's newcomer centers help newly arrived refugee students transition into the school system. Provides intensive ESL, orientation, and counseling. Available at several schools throughout the district.

(619) 522-1000
Free · Public school

San Diego Public Library — Kids & Teens

Free Library Programs

Free programs for children and teens at every library branch — including story time in multiple languages, homework help, teen spaces, and summer reading programs. No documentation needed for library card.

(619) 236-5800
Free · All ages

Women's Programs

Programs designed specifically for refugee women, including safety, empowerment, and community connection.

IRC Women's Empowerment Program

Women's Leadership & Safety

Programs for refugee women including safety planning, economic empowerment, ESL, and community leadership. Women-only spaces available. Addresses domestic violence, gender-based violence, and culturally specific challenges.

(619) 641-7510
Free · Women-only spaces available

National Domestic Violence Hotline

Safety & Crisis Support

24/7 confidential support for anyone experiencing domestic violence, available in many languages. Can connect to local shelters and legal services. Text START to 88788 for text support.

1-800-799-7233 (24/7)
Free · 24/7 · Multilingual

Libraries & Learning Centers

Free resources, internet access, and community programs at every library branch.

San Diego Public Library

Free Library System

36 branches across San Diego County. Free internet access, computers, books in many languages, ESL resources, children's programs, and community meeting spaces. Get a library card for free — no documentation required from most branches.

(619) 236-5800 — Central Library
330 Park Blvd, San Diego (Central)
Free · 36 locations

El Cajon Public Library

Free Library with ESL Programs

Serves El Cajon — a major center of refugee life in San Diego County — with free ESL classes, books in Arabic, Somali, Karen, and other languages, and community programs. Free internet and computer access.

(619) 588-3718
201 E Douglas Ave, El Cajon
Free · Arabic/Somali resources
