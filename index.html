Key Organizations

The organizations that help you most.

These are San Diego's primary refugee resettlement agencies. All are free, all serve all nationalities and religions.

IRC San Diego

International Rescue Committee — full resettlement services: housing, case management, employment, legal, and mental health. Founded 1975.

(619) 641-7510 5348 University Ave, San Diego

Catholic Charities

Refugee & Immigrant Services — legal aid, housing, ESL, job training. Open to all regardless of faith or background.

(619) 287-9454 ext. 2100 4575-A Mission Gorge Pl, San Diego

JFS / HIAS San Diego

Jewish Family Service — comprehensive resettlement, refugee financial coaching, mental health, and English programs. Serves all faiths.

(858) 872-0101 8788 Balboa Ave, San Diego
