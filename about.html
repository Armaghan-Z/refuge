Our Mission

Built with purpose.

The Refuge exists to make life easier for newly arrived families by putting the most critical resources in one accessible place.

Why We Exist

The problem we're solving.

When a refugee family arrives in a new country, they face an overwhelming maze of questions. Where do we live? How do we get legal status? Can my children go to school? Where do I find a doctor who speaks my language?

The answers exist — but they're scattered across dozens of government websites, nonprofit portals, and phone directories. For someone who is new to the country, doesn't yet speak English fluently, and may be dealing with the aftereffects of displacement and trauma, navigating this information alone is incredibly hard.

The Refuge is designed to solve one specific problem: put the most important resources in one place, in plain language, with direct links — so families spend less time searching and more time building their new life.

The Story Behind It

A personal connection.

"

I'm a Pashtun American, and for the past four years I've been tutoring refugee students and their families here in San Diego — often in Pashto. I've sat with families at kitchen tables, helping them fill out forms they couldn't read, explaining what a lease meant, translating medical paperwork, trying to find them a legal clinic that was still accepting new clients.

What struck me every single time was not how complicated the American system is — it's that the information to navigate it exists, but it's nowhere near accessible enough. A family shouldn't have to know three organizations, two websites, and a hotline number just to understand their housing rights.

The Refuge started as a personal project to build the resource I wish I'd had to hand people. It's designed with Pashto and Dari support in mind from the start, because that's the community I know best — but it's meant to serve everyone.

AZ
Armaghan Zarak
High School Senior · San Diego, CA · GitHub: Armaghan-Z
Our Values

What we stand for.

Four pillars that guide every design and content decision on this site.

01

Empowerment

We provide information, not dependency. Every resource here is designed to help people understand and advocate for themselves — not just survive, but thrive.

02

Community

Rebuilding a life is not done alone. We amplify the real organizations in San Diego who have been showing up for refugee families long before this site existed.

03

Accessibility

Language, literacy, and digital access are real barriers. This site is designed to be simple, direct, and usable across six languages — with plain-language explanations throughout.

04

Dignity

Refugees are not problems to be solved. They are people with skills, histories, and futures. This site reflects that at every turn — in its design, its tone, and its content.
