Legal Aid & Immigration

Free or low-cost immigration legal services. Never pay for immigration help without verifying the person is a licensed attorney or accredited representative.

⚠
Warning: Immigration Fraud (Notario Fraud)

Some people pretend to be immigration lawyers but are not. Only use a licensed attorney or an accredited representative. If someone charges $500+ for simple forms, that is likely fraud. Verify at ILRC.org.

Healthcare

Free and sliding-scale medical care. All refugees are encouraged to get a free health screening within 90 days of arrival.

Federally Qualified Health Center with multiple San Diego locations. Sliding-scale fees based on income — many refugees pay nothing. Provides primary care, dental, mental health, and OB/GYN services.

Network of community health centers serving South San Diego and border communities. Accepts Medi-Cal and sliding-scale patients. Services include primary care, dental, vision, and behavioral health.

Free food, food assistance programs, and nutrition support.

CalFresh Eligibility Note (2026)

Federal policy changes (H.R.1) may have affected CalFresh eligibility for some refugees. Verify your current eligibility at BenefitsCal.com before applying. IRC and Catholic Charities can also help you check eligibility.

Education & ESL

Free English classes, GED programs, school enrollment, and vocational training.

📚
Children have the right to attend school

Under Plyler v. Doe (1982), all children have the right to attend public school regardless of immigration status. Contact your local school district to enroll — no documentation required from parents.

San Diego Continuing Education (SDCCE)

Free Adult Education

Completely free adult education programs including ESL (all levels), GED preparation, citizenship test prep, and workforce training. Multiple campuses across San Diego County. No documentation required to enroll.

Transportation

Getting around San Diego using public transit and other options.

MTS (Metropolitan Transit System)

Bus & Trolley

San Diego's bus and trolley network. Get a Compass Card (reloadable transit card) at the MTS store. Plan trips with Google Maps or the MTS trip planner. Low-income riders may qualify for the PRONTO discount program.

511 — trip planning line
MTS Store: 1255 Imperial Ave, San Diego
Pay-per-ride · Discounts available

California AB 60 Driver's License

Driver's License Program

California law (AB 60) allows residents to get a driver's license regardless of immigration status. Visit the San Diego DMV with proof of identity and California residency. Must pass written and driving tests.

(800) 777-0133 — DMV appointment line
3960 Normal St, San Diego (main office)
Open to all immigrants

Financial Services & Banking

Opening a bank account, building credit, and managing money as a newcomer.

Opening a Bank Account (Without SSN)

Banking Access

You can open a bank account without a Social Security Number using: a Refugee Travel Document, I-94 (Arrival/Departure Record), or passport + proof of address. Many banks and credit unions in San Diego accept these. Recommended options: Bank of America Opportunity Checking, Mission Federal Credit Union, California Coast CU.

IRC also offers financial coaching — (619) 641-7510
No SSN required

IRC Financial Coaching

Free Financial Education

IRC offers free one-on-one financial coaching to help refugees open bank accounts, build credit, budget, and work toward financial stability. Available in multiple languages.

(619) 641-7510
Free
