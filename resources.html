Resource Center

Everything you need,
all in one place.

Verified resources for emergency help, housing, legal assistance, healthcare, and education — with real links to trusted organizations.

Emergency Housing Legal Healthcare Education

Need immediate help right now?

Call the USCIS Contact Center at 1-800-375-5283 (available 24/7, with interpreters). For domestic emergencies, call 911. For immediate shelter in San Diego, contact 2-1-1 San Diego (dial 2-1-1) — available around the clock in multiple languages.

Housing Assistance

Emergency Shelter Listings

Searchable database of emergency shelters across the U.S., including San Diego. Filter by population served, availability, and services offered.

shelterlistings.org

HUD Rental Assistance

U.S. Department of Housing and Urban Development rental assistance programs. Find local housing agencies, voucher programs, and affordable housing options.

hud.gov

IRC San Diego — Refugee Housing

The International Rescue Committee in San Diego provides direct housing placement and transition services for newly arrived refugees and asylees.

rescue.org/san-diego

Legal Assistance

Immigration Legal Directory

Find accredited immigration representatives and free or low-cost legal help near you. Searchable by zip code and type of service needed.

immigrationadvocates.org

USCIS — Documentation Help

Official U.S. Citizenship and Immigration Services guides for ID documents, work authorization, travel documents, and asylum applications.

uscis.gov

Jewish Family Service — Legal Aid

JFS San Diego offers free immigration legal services including consultations, applications, and representation for refugees and asylum seekers.

jfssd.org/legal

Healthcare

HRSA — Find a Health Center

Locate federally qualified health centers near you that provide care regardless of ability to pay, including primary care, dental, and mental health services.

findahealthcenter.hrsa.gov

SAMHSA — Mental Health Support

Substance Abuse and Mental Health Services Administration treatment finder — locate mental health counselors and trauma-informed care services near you.

findtreatment.samhsa.gov

Medi-Cal for Refugees

California's Medi-Cal program offers free or low-cost health coverage for refugees and asylum seekers. Apply through Covered California or your county health office.

coveredca.com

Education & Training

ESL / English Classes — San Diego

San Diego Continuing Education offers free English as a Second Language classes throughout the county, with multiple levels, schedules, and locations.

sdce.edu/esl

Job Training — Workforce Partnership

San Diego Workforce Partnership provides free vocational training, certifications, and career development programs for eligible adults including refugees.

workforce.org

Duolingo for Refugees

Free language learning app available in 40+ languages. Refugees can apply for Duolingo English Test fee waivers for university admissions.

duolingo.com

Can't find what you need?

Reach out directly — we'll do our best to connect you with the right resource for your situation.
